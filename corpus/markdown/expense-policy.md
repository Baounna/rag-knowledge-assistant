# Expense Policy

This policy covers reimbursement for business expenses incurred by employees.

## Reimbursement Deadlines

All expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the expense being
incurred. Reports submitted after this window require VP approval. They must
respond within 5 business days.

Expenses older than 90 days will not be reimbursed under any circumstances,
including with VP approval. If you discover an unclaimed expense after this
period, raise it with your manager as a one-off payroll adjustment instead.

## Receipts

Receipts are required for any expense over $25. Photos of receipts are
acceptable provided the merchant name, date, and total are legible. Lost
receipts require a signed declaration form countersigned by your manager.

For recurring subscriptions charged to a personal card, a single monthly
statement extract is sufficient; you do not need to submit twelve identical
receipts.

## Travel

Book flights through the corporate travel portal wherever possible. Economy
class is standard for flights under six hours. Business class requires
director approval and must be requested before booking, not after.

Per diem rates for meals are $60 in the United States, EUR 55 in the euro
area, and GBP 50 in the United Kingdom. Per diem does not require receipts.

### Mileage

Personal vehicle use is reimbursed at $0.67 per mile. Log the journey in the
travel portal with start and end addresses. Commuting between home and your
assigned office is not reimbursable.
